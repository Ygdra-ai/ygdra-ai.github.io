01

The argument

Buying the same AI as everyone else keeps you even with your competitors. It does not keep you ahead. Your own understanding of your own products is the one thing no vendor can sell you.

What sets you apart

A company that engineers and builds to order lives on what it knows: how its products are configured, which rules hold across variants, which supplier part is trusted for which load, how an order really moves from sale to delivery, what was decided and why. Some of it is in the PLM and the ERP. More is in drawings, folders and spreadsheets, and the most valuable part is in the heads of a few experienced people.

02

Three levels of AI

Every engineer can open a general AI model in a browser. Every PLM, ERP and design-tool vendor is building AI into its product.

Both are useful, and a build-to-order manufacturer should use both. Neither sets it apart, because its competitors have the same.

  1. Level 1

    General AI on your data

    A general model works out your business afresh each time, from whatever it is shown. The answers change with the model, the user and the prompt.

  2. Level 2

    AI built into your systems

    It applies the vendor’s logic, the same for every customer. It solves real problems and keeps you even with your competitors.

  3. Level 3

    AI on what only you know

    Agents work from your own understanding, written down and owned. Only the third level puts you ahead.

03

What we do

We write that understanding down and put it to work.

  1. 01Understanding firstNothing is automated before it is understood.
  2. 02Written down with your peopleWhat your people know is written down with them, under their roles. They stay its authors.
  3. 03Held where you decideIn open formats any person and any model can read, beside your PLM, ERP and ECAD, not inside them.
  4. 04One door for agentsWe build it so that every agent is registered before first use and every answer carries its evidence.
  5. 05A person answersA person answers for every release. Agents can take on more of the work; answering for it stays with people.
  6. 06Yours without usSwitch off every agent and every app, and you keep everything that was written down.

04

The test

Four questions. You have context sovereignty when the answer to each is yes.

  1. T1Is the record of your understanding held in storage you control, in open formats?
  2. T2Can any capable model, and any person, read it without a vendor?
  3. T3Do agents reach it only through one controlled door, with every agent registered, every answer evidenced and a person answering for every release?
  4. T4If every agent and app is switched off, is the record still intact?

Take this list to any vendor, including us.
