Context sovereignty.

Your understanding stays yours.

The one AI advantage a competitor cannot buy is your own understanding of your products and your work.We write it down, put it to work, and make sure it stays yours.

01

The argument

Buying the same AI as everyone else keeps you even with your competitors. It does not keep you ahead. Your own understanding of your own products is the one thing no vendor can sell you.

What sets you apart

A company that engineers and builds to order lives on what it knows: how its products are configured, which rules hold across variants, which supplier part is trusted for which load, how an order really moves from sale to delivery, what was decided and why. Some of it is in the PLM and the ERP. More is in drawings, folders and spreadsheets, and the most valuable part is in the heads of a few experienced people. That understanding is the one thing a competitor cannot buy.

02

Three levels of AI

Every engineer can open a general AI model in a browser. Every PLM, ERP and design-tool vendor is building AI into its product.

Both are useful, and a build-to-order manufacturer should use both. Neither sets it apart, because its competitors have the same.

  1. Level 1

    General AI on your data

    A general model works out your business afresh each time, from whatever it is shown. The answers change with the model, the user and the prompt.

  2. Level 2

    AI built into your systems

    It applies the vendor’s logic, the same for every customer. It solves real problems and keeps you even with your competitors.

  3. Level 3

    AI on what only you know

    Agents work from your own understanding, written down and owned. Only the third level puts you ahead.
