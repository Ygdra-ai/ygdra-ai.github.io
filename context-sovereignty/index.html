Context sovereignty

Your understanding stays yours.

In short

Context sovereignty means a company owns the understanding its AI agents work from: what its products are, how its work is done, what was decided and why. That understanding lives in a record the company holds, in open formats any model can read, and agents reach it through one controlled door. Models and agents can be replaced. The understanding stays.

01

Defined

Context sovereignty is a working term, not a standard. We use it for one condition: the context that AI agents reason over belongs to the company, not to the model vendor, not to the agent vendor, and not to the system the data happened to sit in.

“Context” here is the company’s understanding of its own products and its own work: structures, parts, variants, rules, workflows, roles, decisions, the reasons behind them, and the evidence for each. It is not the raw data, not metadata about data, and not the “context window” of a model. It is what an experienced engineer carries in their head and a new one spends years acquiring.

02

The problem it addresses

Three requirements pull against each other.

  1. Use AI on your own products and work now, while the gains are still a lead and not yet the norm.
  2. Keep what the AI works out about your products yours. Otherwise it stays in chat histories, vendor memories and product configurations the company cannot take with it.
  3. Keep every answer auditable. A release signed on the strength of an AI answer needs the evidence behind it, years later.

General AI tools and the AI built into business systems answer the first requirement well. The second and third are left to the company. Context sovereignty is the name for meeting all three. See Competitive advantage levels of AI for why the second one decides who gets ahead.

The point

Models and agents can be replaced. The understanding stays.

03

The test

Four questions. A company has context sovereignty when the answer to each is yes.

  1. T1Is the record of its understanding held in storage the company controls, in open, documented formats?
  2. T2Can any capable model, and any person, read that record, with no vendor needed to make sense of it?
  3. T3Do agents reach it only through one controlled door? A vendor should be able to show that every agent is registered before first use, that every answer carries its evidence, and that a person answers for every release.
  4. T4If every agent and every app is switched off, is the record still intact and usable, ready for whatever agents come next?

Take this list to any vendor, including us.

04

How it relates to MCP

Context sovereignty is a governance and ownership practice, not a new protocol. The door it calls for can be a Model Context Protocol server. MCP is the open standard for connecting models to tools and data; it says nothing about who owns the context behind the door.

05

Elsewhere

The term appears in AI writing and vendor glossaries, usually meaning that sensitive data stays within an organisation’s jurisdiction while governed context is served to models. Nordic readers will also hear the EU debate on cloud and data sovereignty, which is about where data is hosted. Our use is narrower and stronger on one point: the record itself is portable and owned, not merely kept in place.
