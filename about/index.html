About Ygdra

01

What Ygdra does

Ygdra helps industrial manufacturers that engineer and build to order write their own understanding of their products and their work into a record they own.

The record sits beside their PLM, ERP and ECAD systems, in open formats, so that AI agents can work from it through one controlled door. Read Context sovereignty for the idea and Competitive advantage levels of AI for why it matters.

About the name

Ygdra is named after the world tree: one structure, many worlds, three roots that drink from three wells. The spelling is ours. You need none of this to work with us. What you need, in the age of AI, is to keep your own understanding of your own products yours. That is the whole idea.
