Questions

What industrial manufacturers ask first, answered briefly.

  1. Q-01

    Do we have to replace our PLM or ERP?

    No. Beside, not instead.

    The record sits beside your PLM, ERP and ECAD systems, not inside them and not in their place. Nothing moves: they stay where they are and keep doing what they do. The record adds what only you know.

  2. Q-02

    Should we stop using general AI tools, or the AI in our systems?

    No. Levels 1 and 2 stay useful.

    General AI tools are level 1, and the AI your PLM, ERP and design-tool vendors build into their products is level 2. Keep using both where they fit. Neither sets you apart, because your competitors have the same. Your own understanding, written down and owned, is what adds level 3.

  3. Q-03

    Why not just buy what the vendors offer?

    Because everyone else can buy it too.

    What a vendor sells is level 2: the same logic for every customer. It keeps you even with your competitors; your own understanding is what puts you ahead.

    Context sovereignty →

  4. Q-04

    You are a small company. What if you shut down?

    Then you keep everything that was written down.

    It is in open formats, in storage you control, readable by any model. That is the point of context sovereignty.

  5. Q-05

    AI changes every month. Won’t this be out of date in a year?

    Models and agents are replaceable by design.

    The record is not tied to any of them.

  6. Q-06

    Is this replacing our engineers?

    No. Agents take steps, not responsibility.

    What your people know is written down with them, under their roles; they stay its authors, and a person answers for every release.

  7. Q-07

    Does this compete with the AI our PLM partner sells?

    No. That is level 2, and you should use it.

    The record sits beside it and adds what only you know.
