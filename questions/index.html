Questions

What industrial manufacturers ask first, answered briefly.

  1. Q-01

    Do we have to replace our PLM or ERP?

    No. Beside, not instead.

    The record sits beside your PLM, ERP and ECAD systems, not inside them and not in their place. Nothing moves: they stay where they are and keep doing what they do. The record adds what only you know.

  2. Q-02

    Should we stop using general AI tools, or the AI in our systems?

    No. Levels 1 and 2 stay useful.

    General AI tools are level 1, and the AI your PLM, ERP and design-tool vendors build into their products is level 2. Keep using both where they fit. Neither sets you apart, because your competitors have the same. Your own understanding, written down and owned, is what adds level 3.

    Competitive advantage levels of AI

  3. Q-03

    Why not just buy what the vendors offer?

    Because everyone else can buy it too.

    Buying the same AI as everyone else keeps you even with your competitors. It does not keep you ahead. Your own understanding of your own products is the one thing no vendor can sell you.
