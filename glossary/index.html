Glossary

One definition per term, so that every text uses them the same way. Terms marked “own term” are ones we define and intend to be known for.

  1. G-01

    Context sovereignty

    Own term

    A company owns the understanding its AI agents work from, holds it in a record it controls, in open formats any model can read, and lets agents reach it only through one controlled door. Models and agents can be replaced; the understanding stays. A working term, not a standard.

    The long entry →

  2. G-02

    Competitive advantage levels of AI

    Own term

    Three levels at which a company can use AI. Level 1, general AI on your data: the model works out the business afresh each time. Level 2, AI built into your systems: the vendor’s logic, the same for every customer, so it keeps a company even with its competitors. Level 3, AI on what only you know: the company’s own understanding, recorded and owned, the only level a competitor cannot buy. The levels build on each other.

    The long entry →

  3. G-03

    Compounding context

    Own term

    A company’s written-down understanding grows fuller with every piece of work that adds to it, and each later piece of work starts from everything the earlier ones left. It is why the record is worth more each year while the agents that use it stay replaceable.

  4. G-04

    Tacit knowledge

    What people know and do without it being written anywhere: why a variant is built this way, which check catches the costly error. “We can know more than we can tell,” in Michael Polanyi’s phrase (The Tacit Dimension, 1966). Writing it down, with the people who hold it and under their roles, is what knowledge research calls externalisation (Nonaka and Takeuchi, 1995). They remain its owners.

  5. G-05

    The record

    Own term

    A company’s written-down understanding of its products and its work: structures, variants, rules, decisions, and the reasons and evidence behind them. It is owned by the company, held where the company decides, in open formats any person and any model can read, beside its PLM, ERP and ECAD systems and not inside them. Switch off every agent and every app, and the record stays.

  6. G-06

    One door

    Own term

    The single, controlled way AI agents reach the record. A door built this way registers every agent before first use, makes every answer carry its evidence, and leaves a person answering for every release. It can be a Model Context Protocol server: MCP connects models to tools and data, and says nothing about who owns what is behind the door.
